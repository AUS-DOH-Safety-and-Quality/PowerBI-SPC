title: "X-Axis Aesthetics"

All settings related to formatting the x-axis are available in this section. These are grouped by the four categories, Axis, Ticks, Label and Gridlines.

Please note that although the axis **Lower Limit** and **Upper Limit** truncate the SPC chart, the centerline and limits are not recalculated and will be based on all the data available.
